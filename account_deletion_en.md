# Deleting Your Dripsight Account and Data

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account, and the data stored on the Developer's server, for the Android app "Dripsight" (developer: yuzumaruca) distributed on Google Play.

- App name: Dripsight
- Developer: yuzumaruca
- Contact: matsubara20201021@gmail.com

## 1. About accounts in Dripsight

- You can use the App without signing in. Recording, master data and analysis do not need an account.
- An **anonymous account that does not use your name or email address** (a random identifier) is created only when you do one of the following: use the AI feature for the first time, redeem a promo code, create or join a shared group, view the contents of a share key, or import shared contents.
- Signing in with Google is required only when you purchase the Pro plan or restore a purchase. At that point the anonymous account is linked to your Google account.
- If you have done none of the above, no account has been created and no data is stored on our server.

## 2. Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open "Settings" in the app.
2. Under "Privacy", choose "Delete account and cloud data".
3. On the "Delete account" screen, review what will and will not be deleted.
4. Tick "I understand the above" and press "Delete account".
5. Choose "Delete" in the "Delete your account?" dialog to carry out the deletion. When it is complete, "Your account and cloud data have been deleted" is shown.

Notes:

- If no account has been created on the device, the screen shows "No account has been created on this device. No data is stored on our server." No action is needed in that case.
- If "Couldn't delete." is shown, check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, contact us at the address in Section 3.
- **Your Pro subscription is not canceled when you delete your account.** Please cancel it in Google Play first. "Open Google Play subscriptions" on the "Delete account" screen opens the Google Play subscriptions page.
- If you use the AI feature or similar features after deleting, a new anonymous account is created. Data from the deleted account will not come back.

## 3. If you cannot use the app (for example, you have uninstalled it)

Please email the address below with the subject "Account deletion request".

- Contact: matsubara20201021@gmail.com
- **If you signed in with Google (purchased the Pro plan)**: include the email address of the Google account you signed in with. To confirm your identity, please send the request from that Google account's email address where possible. If you write from a different address, we may contact you to confirm your identity.
- **Deadline**: We will delete the data, after confirming your identity, within 30 days of receiving your request, and let you know by email when it is done.

**If you used only an anonymous account and have already uninstalled the App**: the account identifier was kept only inside the App, so we cannot identify your data and cannot delete it individually. In this case the following information, linked to a random identifier, may remain on our server. None of it includes information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

- AI usage counts and promo code redemption records
- Cloud backups (if you used the Pro plan through a promo code)
- Share links you created and records you contributed to a shared group

We recommend deleting your account using the steps in Section 2 before uninstalling the App.

## 4. Data that will be deleted

The following data is deleted when you delete in the App, or when we handle your deletion request. Deleted data cannot be restored.

- Your account (anonymous, or linked with Google)
- Cloud backups (record data and photos)
- Your membership in shared groups and the records you contributed (if you created the group, its management passes to another member; if you were the last member, the group itself is deleted)
- Share links you created (including the shared contents)
- AI usage counts, Pro plan status, and promo code redemption records
- Purchase records (those stored on our server)

## 5. Data that is not deleted, and retention periods

| Data | Handling |
|---|---|
| Records, master data, analysis results, photos, brewing articles and settings on your device | These are stored on your device separately from your account, so deleting your account does not remove them. **They are removed when you uninstall the App.** You can also delete individual records and master data from the App's screens |
| Temporary records kept for abuse prevention (AI request records, records used to grant free usage after watching ads, notifications from Google Play, daily counts based on hashed IP addresses) | Automatically deleted after 24 hours to 90 days depending on the type (**90 days at most**) |
| Previous versions of cloud backups | After deletion, earlier versions may remain for up to 30 days and then be kept for up to 7 more days for internal recovery before being permanently deleted. You cannot restore them from the App during this time |
| Server access logs (identifier, feature name, response time, result, source IP address) | Automatically deleted according to Google Cloud's retention settings |
| Usage statistics and error reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) | These are linked to an identifier for each installation of the App, not to your account, so they are not covered by account deletion. Usage statistics are automatically deleted after 14 months, and error reports after Google's default retention period. You can stop further sending by turning off "Share usage and error reports" under "Privacy" in Settings |
| Your Google Play subscription, purchase history and payment records | These are managed by Google, and the Developer cannot delete them. Please cancel your subscription in Google Play. Purchase history is handled under Google's privacy policy |
